Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 21 November 2026

VID STATUS MAKER is an app that turns your photos into video status clips (developer: Vidyut Creator). This page explains in plain words what data the app uses and what it does not. A simple Hinglish version follows below.

1. Your photos, videos and music

Your photos, videos and music are processed on your own phone. They are never uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them.

The videos you create stay on your phone (in the app's Saved section). They go to your Gallery only when you tap Download or Save. When you share, the file goes only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp).

2. What we do not collect

The app has no account, login or sign-up. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, or camera or microphone data.

3. Why the app uses the internet

To fetch the template list and template files, and to read a small online settings file (settings.json) that switches ads on or off and can update the app logo. Only template and settings files are downloaded; none of your content is sent. These files are hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host can see your IP address in a normal request.

Templates you have already downloaded keep working offline.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and may show Google AdMob ads (one banner ad, and one full-screen ad before a video export starts). Ads are controlled by an online setting, so at times the app shows no ads. When ads are on, AdMob may use certain device information (such as the Advertising ID, device type and approximate location) to serve and measure ads. This data goes to Google, not to us.

When ads are on and where required by law (for example in the EU/UK), the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice later from the Help page (where this option applies). You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings. Google's page: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Permissions

Internet and network state (for templates, settings and ads), and the Advertising ID (for ads). Photos, videos or music are read only when you pick a file yourself. The app does not ask for camera, microphone, location or storage permission.

6. Outside links

The app has links to our Telegram, YouTube and Instagram pages. Opening them takes you to those apps or websites, which have their own privacy policies.

7. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored inside it (Saved videos, cached templates). Videos you saved to your Gallery are yours and can be deleted from the Gallery.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will be shown at the top.

10. Contact

For any question, contact us by email at vidyutcreator@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy (Hinglish)

Last updated: 21 November 2026

VID STATUS MAKER ek photo se WhatsApp status video banane wala app hai (developer: Vidyut Creator). Ye page simple bhasha me batata hai ki app tumhara kya data use karta hai aur kya nahi. English version is page me upar di gayi hai.

1. Tumhari photos aur videos

Tumhari photos, videos aur music tumhare phone par hi process hoti hain. Koi bhi photo, video ya music hamare kisi server par upload nahi hoti aur hum unhe dekh nahi sakte.

Jo video tum banate ho wo sirf tumhare phone me (app ke andar Saved section me) rehti hai. Gallery me tabhi jaati hai jab tum khud Download ya Save dabate ho. Share karne par wo sirf usi app ko jaati hai jo tum chunte ho (jaise WhatsApp).

2. Hum kya collect nahi karte

App me koi account, login ya signup nahi hai. Hum tumhara naam, phone number, email, location, contacts, camera ya microphone ka data nahi lete.

3. Internet kyun use hota hai

Naye templates ki list aur template files download karne ke liye, aur ek chhoti online settings file (settings.json) padhne ke liye jisse ads on/off hote hain aur app ka logo/naam badal sakta hai. Isme sirf template aur settings files aati hain, tumhari koi jaankari upar nahi jaati. Ye files GitHub Pages par rakhi hain. Jaise har website ya server ko normal request ke saath tumhara IP address dikhta hai, waise hi unhe bhi dikh sakta hai.

Internet band ho to pehle download ho chuke templates offline chalte hain.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

App free hai. App me Google AdMob ke ads dikh sakte hain (ek banner ad aur video export se pehle ek full-screen ad). Ads online settings se on/off hote hain, isliye kabhi app me ads nahi bhi dikhte. Jab ads on hote hain, AdMob tumhare device ki kuch jaankari (jaise advertising ID, device ka type, approx. jagah) use kar sakta hai taaki ads dikha sake aur unhe sahi se naap sake. Ye data Google ko jaata hai, hume nahi.

Ads on hone par, jahan kanoon ke hisaab se zaroori hai (jaise EU/UK), app Google ka consent form dikhata hai. Tum baad me Help page par jaake Ad privacy settings se apni choice badal sakte ho (jahan ye option laagu ho). Tum phone ki Settings me advertising ID reset ya delete bhi kar sakte ho.

Google ka privacy page: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Permissions

Internet aur network state (templates, settings aur ads ke liye), aur advertising ID (ads ke liye). Photos, video ya music tabhi padhi jaati hain jab tum khud file chunte ho. Camera, microphone, location ya storage ki permission app nahi maangta.

6. Bahar ke links

App me hamare Telegram, YouTube aur Instagram ke links hain. Unhe kholne par tum Telegram, YouTube ya Instagram ke app/website par chale jaate ho, jinki apni privacy policy hoti hai.

7. Bachche

App 13 saal se chhote bachchon ke liye nahi hai aur hum jaankar unka data collect nahi karte.

8. Data delete karna

App uninstall karte hi app ke andar ka sara data (Saved videos, cache kiye templates) hat jaata hai. Jo videos tumne Gallery me save ki hain wo tumhari hain, unhe tum Gallery se delete kar sakte ho.

9. Badlav

Ye policy badli to naya date upar likha jayega aur naya version app me dikhega.

10. Sampark

Koi sawal ho to email par: vidyutcreator@gmail.com sampark karo.
