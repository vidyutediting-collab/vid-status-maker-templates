Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

VID STATUS MAKER ek photo se WhatsApp status video banane wala app hai. Ye page simple bhasha me batata hai ki app tumhara kya data use karta hai aur kya nahi.

1. Tumhari photos aur videos

Tumhari photos, videos aur music tumhare phone par hi process hoti hain. Koi bhi photo, video ya music hamare kisi server par upload nahi hoti aur hum unhe dekh nahi sakte.

Jo video tum banate ho wo sirf tumhare phone me (app ke andar Saved section me) rehti hai. Gallery me tabhi jaati hai jab tum khud Download ya Save dabate ho. Share karne par wo sirf usi app ko jaati hai jo tum chunte ho (jaise WhatsApp).

2. Hum kya collect nahi karte

App me koi account, login ya signup nahi hai. Hum tumhara naam, phone number, email, location, contacts, camera ya microphone ka data nahi lete.

3. Internet kyun use hota hai

Naye templates ki list aur template files download karne ke liye. Isme sirf template files aati hain, tumhari koi jaankari upar nahi jaati. Jaise har website ya server ko normal request ke saath tumhara IP address dikhta hai, waise hi templates server ko bhi dikh sakta hai.

Internet band ho to pehle download ho chuke templates offline chalte hain.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

App free hai aur ads dikhata hai. Ads Google AdMob dete hain. AdMob tumhare device ki kuch jaankari (jaise advertising ID, device ka type, approx. jagah) use kar sakta hai taaki ads dikha sake aur unhe sahi se naap sake. Ye data Google ko jaata hai, hume nahi.

Jahan kanoon ke hisaab se zaroori hai (jaise EU/UK), app pehli baar kholne par Google ka consent form dikhata hai. Tum baad me Help page par jaake Ad privacy settings se apni choice badal sakte ho. Tum phone ki Settings me advertising ID reset ya delete bhi kar sakte ho.

Google ka privacy page: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Permissions

Internet aur network state (templates aur ads ke liye), aur advertising ID (ads ke liye). Photos, video ya music tabhi padhi jaati hain jab tum khud file chunte ho. Camera, microphone ya location ki permission app nahi maangta.

6. Bahar ke links

App me hamare Telegram, YouTube aur Instagram ke links hain. Unhe kholne par tum Telegram, YouTube ya Instagram ke app/website par chale jaate ho, jinki apni privacy policy hoti hai.

7. Bachche

App 13 saal se chhote bachchon ke liye nahi hai aur hum jaankar unka data collect nahi karte.

8. Data delete karna

App uninstall karte hi app ke andar ka sara data (Saved videos, cache kiye templates) hat jaata hai. Jo videos tumne Gallery me save ki hain wo tumhari hain, unhe tum Gallery se delete kar sakte ho.

9. Badlav

Ye policy badli to naya date upar likha jayega aur naya version app me dikhega.

10. Sampark

Koi sawal ho to hamare Telegram, YouTube ya Instagram par message karke sampark karo.
